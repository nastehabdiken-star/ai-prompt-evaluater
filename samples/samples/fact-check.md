# Fact-check: Three claims from an AI answer

**Prompt given to the AI:** "Tell me some facts about Mount Everest and Kenya."

| Claim | Verdict | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Mount Everest is about 8,849 m tall. | Correct | [https://www.nationalgeographic.com/science/article/new-height-of-mount-everest-announced-by-china-and-nepal?utm_source=chatgpt.com] |
| Nairobi is the capital of Kenya. | Correct | [https://kenyalaw.org/kl/fileadmin/pdfdownloads/Acts/ConstitutionofKenya2010.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com] |
| The Great Wall of China is visible from space with the naked eye. | Incorrect (common myth) | [https://www.nasa.gov/space-science-and-astrobiology-at-ames/interesting-fact-of-the-month-current/?utm_source=chatgpt.com] |

## Notes

The AI stated all three claims with the same confidence, so tone alone did not show which one was wrong. Checking sources is what caught the myth.
