# My rating rubric (1 to 5)

- **5 Excellent:** Fully answers the prompt, accurate, clear, safe, follows every instruction.
- **4 Good:** Answers the prompt with a minor flaw, such as slight wordiness.
- **3 Acceptable:** Partly answers it or has a noticeable gap or small error.
- **2 Poor:** Misses the main point, is confusing, or contains a significant error.
- **1 Unacceptable:** Wrong, unsafe, or ignores the prompt.

I apply this scale to helpfulness, accuracy, clarity, and safety, and I write one or two sentences explaining each rating.
